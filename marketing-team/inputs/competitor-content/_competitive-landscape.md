## Direct competitors (1:1 career/PM mentorship, PT-BR)

### Diego Eis — STRONGEST OVERLAP
- Source: https://diegoeis.com/mentoria/
- Headline: "Mentoria individual para Product Managers e Líderes de produtos"
- Format: subscription, 1-2 sessions/month; includes content library ("segundo cérebro").
- Audience: PMs and product leaders, junior → C-level, plus transitions (design, data).
- Positioning: experience-exchange ("troca"), not top-down instruction. Explicit: "não quero que você terceirize suas decisões comigo." Holistic product + business + leadership. Pitches unconventional views vs LinkedIn mainstream.
- **Overlap risk:** his "autonomy, don't outsource your decisions" line is near-identical to our "autonomia, não dependência." We must differentiate on the open water below, or we read as a clone.
- **Our differentiation vs Diego Eis:**
  1. Active Amazon Senior PM (in the machine now) vs veteran/content-creator stance.
  2. A named, reusable method — Decision Canvas — vs general experience-sharing.
  3. Focus on the career DECISION itself (stay/leave/transition), not product craft tutoring.
  4. Productized offers with explicit variable pricing vs flat subscription.

### PM3
- Source: https://pm3.com.br/blog/conheca-as-mentorias-em-grupo-da-pm3/
- Format: group mentorias + paid courses. Large brand, scale play.
- Position vs us: "formação/curso" (training) more than 1:1 career decision. We win on personalization + senior practitioner; they win on brand + price-per-seat at scale.

## Strategic takeaway for content
The crowded lane is "PM career/craft advice." The open lane — and our wedge — is **career DECISIONS as a product problem** (Decision Canvas, explicit trade-offs), delivered by an active Big Tech operator. Content should keep hammering the decision-method angle, because that's the one positioning Diego Eis and the platforms don't own.
